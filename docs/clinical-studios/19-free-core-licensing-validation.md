# MEDCOEL — legal-use validation for the free core (21 November 2026)

**Status: evidence-based preliminary licensing assessment, NOT a legal opinion, license grant, clinical approval, or release authorization.** No source, resource, rights record, exact build, receipt, quarantine rule or reviewer decision is upgraded by this document. Repository resource files in `local-data/` are private and cannot be independently inspected merely from GitHub. Validate the actual exact-hash files and obligations before any publication.

## Confirmed MEDCOEL use case

The owner states that **MEDCOEL's core platform is free to all users**, including all 24 Clinical Studios, Anatomy Atlas, Anatomy Live 4D Lab and practice questions. No subscription or paywall applies to those core features. A **possible future optional paid product** consists only of separately produced explanatory videos for question discussions. This does not authorize an existing premium offering or commercialization of any third-party dataset. Keep premium video assets, entitlements, credits, provenance and dependency trees separate; never place third-party restricted images/models inside paid videos unless that use is expressly licensed.

**A free-to-view app is not inherently "NonCommercial" under Creative Commons, and a "research only" grant is not a general educational redistribution grant.** The relevant legal scope depends on the exact licensed activity and all related revenue/benefit. Avoid NC/research-only assets in any public MEDCOEL product without rights-holder permission or a qualified review specific to this use, even if the core stays free. Authoritative explanation: https://creativecommons.org/faq/ and https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/licensing-considerations/version4/.

## Distinguish four gates

1. **License-level reuse permission:** Read the exact version's legal text, scope, attribution and ShareAlike obligations. Attribution is not permission for sources that forbid the use. A public download is not a public-domain grant.
2. **Exact-file source/derivative rights:** Preserve origin, checksums, every third-party contribution and any embedded restrictions. A dataset-level license may not automatically override a component's separately held copyright.
3. **Privacy and clinical review:** A CC license does not itself clear patient privacy, burned-in identifiers, medical labels, case keys, misleading interpretations or anatomical correctness.
4. **Installed release approval:** Preserve B1 rights verification, clinical/medical/privacy reviewer decisions, immutable manifest, signed receipt/attestation, and canonical catalog's fail-closed status. Never use owner override on new Clinical Studios.

### Important independent software finding — legacy Atlas / 4D

The existing `atlas-code` rights record and `apps/anatomy/package.json` identify upstream Atlas frontend software as **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, separate from the BodyParts3D mesh terms. Even if the frontend is offered free, this does **not** grant automatic approval for any use connected to future premium content or outside the license's NC scope. Legacy owner override is an operational risk acknowledgment, **not** legal clearance. Choose either (a) written upstream license covering the exact code and MEDCOEL use, or (b) a documented **clean-room replacement** with license-compatible dependencies. This issue cannot be solved by DBCLS granting model rights.

### Brain MRI replacement option — Harvard–Oxford

Oxford's authoritative license page expressly states that the **Harvard–Oxford and Cerebellum atlas datasets** have **CC BY-SA 4.0** terms, *separate from FSL software*, most of which uses a noncommercial license: https://fsl.fmrib.ox.ac.uk/fsl/docs/license.html (Standard Space Atlases section). Therefore the atlas **data** is a plausible alternative; **do not package FSL software** merely to extract it. Before integrating: obtain atlas files from an authorized source, verify their notices and credits, preserve the appropriate license for derivatives, check alignment with the SRI24 MRI and labels, test left/right, and obtain medical/terminology review. Do not import a replacement atlas into public bundles or mark it approved based solely on this paragraph.

## Publication and attribution obligations

For each approved exact release, generate a **public credits page** that includes (as the license requires): dataset title, creators and source URL/DOI, license name/link, adaptation/transformation statement, provenance/version and citation, as well as medical educational limitations. **CC BY-SA derivatives may need to be made available under the applicable same/compatible license**, with no inconsistent technological or legal restrictions. A login or copyright notice must not silently remove the reuser's licensed permissions; coordinate secure review delivery with the distribution rights analysis.

Draft example only, not yet public:

License and required credit must match the grant verified for the exact OBJ release (CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan). MEDCOEL conversions include grouping per region and OBJ-to-GLB transformation. See the per-asset source/NOTICE and license for complete terms.

For the eventual free-core site, make premium explanatory videos a distinct **content product** with its own rights ledger and no dependency on restricted medical images, derivative meshes or noncommercial code. A separate route or repository is useful for auditing but **is not, by itself, a legal guarantee of noncommercial treatment**.
