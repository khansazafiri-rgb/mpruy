# Assets requiring written permission or legal review

Quarantined items listed here are withheld from local builds. The remaining Atlas and 4D Lab builds
are also blocked from **public staging** by `commercial-release.json` until their rights are documented.
Each quarantined item stays withheld until the
written evidence in the right-hand column is on file, after which the item can be released by
editing `quarantine-policy.json` in a reviewed PR. The guard, tests and pipeline all follow the policy.
