# Z-Anatomy publication boundary — 2026-11-25

The pinned Z-Anatomy model archive and its License.txt describe CC BY-SA 4.0, attribution, and derivative sharing. The same notice identifies third-party contributions with NC restrictions and unresolved rights. Source-wide terms do not establish the exact provenance of all 2,338 exported objects.

The publisher's account responded to issue #13 on 2026-10-23: it regards a model-reading application as part of the derivative solution and expects the code to be shared. It could not provide further source details for some third-party contributions. See https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy/issues/13#issuecomment-6361027075 . This is the publisher's stated interpretation; it is not a binding judicial determination of whether independently authored software is derivative.

Issue #10 discusses unclear brain/white-matter provenance and links a document. The linked document could not be inspected in this review; do not treat material names or collection labels as conclusive clearance. https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy/issues/10#issuecomment-6145411656

Decision for this branch: retain all Z-Anatomy originals in quarantine. Do not approve the 2,231 non-priority candidates merely because a name filter did not flag them. Do not approve the 107 priority candidates without exact source mapping or applicable permission. The owner must decide whether to pursue compliant ShareAlike distribution and source-code sharing after legal review, obtain explicit written terms, or use independent alternatives. No permission is claimed on the owner's behalf.

Independent alternative: DBCLS BodyParts3D release 4.0 direct source files (current CC BY 4.0 terms dated 2025-02-27; retain historical embedded notices), plus separately framed HuBMAP HRA reference objects with version-specific metadata. https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html and https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/data/bodyparts3d/LATEST/README_e.html . A new independent export must preserve attribution, source hashes, changes, and clinical/release review. This is not a full-body completeness claim.

Do not merge, activate, or bypass licensing/commercial-release.json; neither legacy Atlas nor 4D Lab is cleared by this review.
