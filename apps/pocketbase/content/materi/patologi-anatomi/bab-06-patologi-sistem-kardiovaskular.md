## Peta Besar Patologi Kardiovaskular

Patologi kardiovaskular paling mudah dipahami dengan empat pertanyaan:

1. Apakah masalah utamanya pada pembuluh darah?
2. Apakah miokard mengalami iskemia, hipertrofi, atau inflamasi?
3. Apakah katup mengalami stenosis, regurgitasi, atau infeksi?
4. Apakah gangguan akhirnya menimbulkan gagal jantung, aritmia, trombus, atau emboli?

Pola besar:

- Atherosclerosis → plaque rupture → thrombosis → myocardial infarction.
- Hypertension → pressure overload → concentric LV hypertrophy.
- Volume overload → chamber dilation + eccentric hypertrophy.
- Valve stenosis → pressure overload.
- Valve regurgitation → volume overload.
- Chronic left-sided heart failure → pulmonary congestion.
- Chronic right-sided heart failure → systemic venous congestion.

## Atherosclerosis — Konsep Dasar

Atherosclerosis adalah penyakit intima arteri besar dan sedang yang ditandai pembentukan atheromatous plaque.

Lokasi yang sering:

- abdominal aorta,
- coronary arteries,
- popliteal arteries,
- internal carotid arteries,
- Circle of Willis.

Komponen plaque:

- fibrous cap,
- necrotic lipid core,
- cholesterol clefts,
- macrophage-derived foam cells,
- smooth muscle cells,
- extracellular matrix,
- kadang calcification.

![Struktur atherosclerotic plaque & komplikasinya](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/10Cc3-ArXmGhGYY4a3bwzjR2UstAyqh6F)

## Faktor Risiko Atherosclerosis

Faktor risiko mayor yang dapat dimodifikasi:

- hyperlipidemia, terutama LDL tinggi,
- hypertension,
- smoking,
- diabetes mellitus.

Faktor tidak dapat dimodifikasi:

- usia,
- sex biologis,
- riwayat keluarga/genetik.

Faktor tambahan:

- inflammation,
- obesity/metabolic syndrome,
- sedentary lifestyle,
- lipoprotein(a) pada sebagian pasien.

> [!highyield] Exam pearl
> Kombinasi smoking + HTN + hyperlipidemia + diabetes = paket klasik faktor risiko atherosclerosis.

## Response-to-Injury Hypothesis

Urutan patogenesis:

1. Endothelial dysfunction/injury.
2. LDL masuk ke intima dan mengalami modifikasi/oxidation.
3. Monocyte adhesion dan migration.
4. Monocyte → macrophage → foam cell.
5. Platelet adhesion dan mediator pertumbuhan.
6. Smooth muscle migration dari media ke intima.
7. Smooth muscle proliferation + ECM deposition.
8. Terbentuk fibrofatty plaque.

Fatty streak adalah lesi awal dan dapat ditemukan sejak usia muda, tetapi tidak semuanya berkembang menjadi plaque klinis bermakna.

## Stable vs Vulnerable Plaque

Stable plaque:

- fibrous cap tebal,
- lipid core relatif kecil,
- inflammation lebih sedikit,
- cenderung menyebabkan stenosis progresif.

Vulnerable plaque:

- fibrous cap tipis,
- lipid core besar,
- banyak inflammatory cells,
- lebih mudah rupture/erosion.

**Clinical link**: plaque rupture/erosion → exposure thrombogenic material → acute thrombosis → acute coronary syndrome.

## Komplikasi Atherosclerotic Plaque

Komplikasi:

- progressive luminal stenosis,
- plaque rupture,
- superficial erosion,
- intraplaque hemorrhage,
- superimposed thrombosis,
- atheroembolism,
- weakening media → aneurysm.

## Ischemic Heart Disease — Spektrum

Ischemic heart disease terjadi karena ketidakseimbangan kebutuhan oksigen miokard dan perfusi koroner.

Spektrum:

- stable angina,
- unstable angina,
- myocardial infarction,
- chronic ischemic heart disease,
- sudden cardiac death.

Penyebab tersering = coronary atherosclerosis.

## Stable Angina vs Acute Coronary Syndrome

Stable angina:

- fixed stenosis,
- gejala saat exertion/stress,
- tidak ada myocardial necrosis.

Acute coronary syndrome:

- biasanya akibat plaque disruption + thrombosis,
- dapat berupa unstable angina, NSTEMI, atau STEMI.

> [!highyield] Exam pearl
> - angina = ischemia tanpa necrosis.
> - MI = ischemia dengan necrosis.

## Myocardial Infarction — Patogenesis

**Urutan klasik**: atherosclerotic plaque disruption → platelet activation → acute thrombosis → coronary occlusion → prolonged ischemia → coagulative necrosis of myocardium.

![MI timeline: ischemia → necrosis → inflammation → scar](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1baj5gG0bcJLREzNgQmXCUDwsfNcR1fS4)

## Transmural vs Subendocardial Infarct

Transmural infarct:

- melibatkan full thickness atau sebagian besar ketebalan dinding,
- sering terkait complete coronary occlusion,
- klasik berkaitan dengan STEMI.

Subendocardial infarct:

- mengenai inner 1/3–1/2 myocardium,
- wilayah subendocardial paling rentan terhadap hypoperfusion,
- dapat terjadi pada partial occlusion atau global hypotension,
- sering berkaitan dengan NSTEMI.

## Distribusi Coronary Infarct

LAD:

- anterior LV,
- anterior septum,
- apex.

RCA:

- inferior/posterior LV pada banyak individu,
- posterior septum tergantung dominance.

LCX:

- lateral LV.

**Practical pearl**: anterior MI besar → pikirkan LAD.

## Morfologi MI — Timeline High-Yield {highyield}

0–4 jam:

- gross minimal,
- perubahan mikroskopik dapat sangat subtle.

4–24 jam:

- early coagulative necrosis,
- edema/hemorrhage,
- mulai neutrophils.

1–3 hari:

- extensive coagulative necrosis,
- dense neutrophilic infiltrate.

3–7 hari:

- macrophages remove necrotic tissue,
- myocardium paling lemah → rupture risk tinggi.

1–2 minggu:

- granulation tissue.

>2 minggu hingga bulan:

- collagen deposition dan scar maturation.

**Exam pearl paling penting**: 3–7 hari = macrophages + wall rupture risk.

## Komplikasi MI

Komplikasi:

- arrhythmia,
- acute LV failure/cardiogenic shock,
- papillary muscle dysfunction/rupture,
- ventricular septal rupture,
- free wall rupture → hemopericardium/tamponade,
- pericarditis,
- mural thrombus,
- ventricular aneurysm,
- chronic heart failure.

## Free Wall, Septal, dan Papillary Muscle Rupture

Free wall rupture:

- hemopericardium,
- cardiac tamponade,
- sudden death.

Septal rupture:

- acute VSD,
- new harsh systolic murmur,
- left-to-right shunt.

Papillary muscle rupture:

- acute severe mitral regurgitation,
- pulmonary edema,
- posteromedial papillary muscle lebih rentan karena single blood supply pada banyak orang.

## Post-MI Pericarditis

Early fibrinous pericarditis dapat terjadi beberapa hari setelah transmural MI akibat inflammation yang mencapai epicardium.

Dressler syndrome:

- autoimmune pericarditis,
- muncul beberapa minggu setelah MI,
- dapat disertai fever dan pericardial effusion.

## Heart Failure — Konsep

Heart failure = jantung tidak mampu memenuhi kebutuhan perfusi jaringan atau hanya mampu dengan peningkatan filling pressure.

Klasifikasi praktis:

- left-sided vs right-sided,
- systolic vs diastolic,
- acute vs chronic.

## Left-Sided Heart Failure

Penyebab:

- ischemic heart disease,
- hypertension,
- aortic/mitral valve disease,
- cardiomyopathy.

Konsekuensi pulmonary venous congestion:

- pulmonary edema,
- dyspnea,
- orthopnea,
- paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea.

Morphology paru kronik:

- hemosiderin-laden macrophages = heart failure cells,
- brown induration pada congestion kronik.

## Right-Sided Heart Failure

Penyebab tersering = left-sided heart failure.

Penyebab lain:

- pulmonary hypertension,
- chronic lung disease → cor pulmonale,
- tricuspid/pulmonic valve disease.

Manifestasi:

- peripheral edema,
- hepatomegaly,
- congestive hepatopathy,
- ascites,
- jugular venous distention.

## Cor Pulmonale

Cor pulmonale = right ventricular hypertrophy/dilation akibat pulmonary hypertension yang berasal dari penyakit paru/pulmonary vasculature, bukan akibat primary left heart disease.

Penyebab:

- COPD,
- interstitial lung disease,
- recurrent pulmonary emboli,
- pulmonary vascular disorders.

## Hypertensive Heart Disease

Systemic hypertension → chronic pressure overload → concentric LV hypertrophy.

Morphology:

- increased LV wall thickness,
- myocyte hypertrophy,
- “boxcar nuclei” pada cardiomyocytes.

Jangka panjang:

- diastolic dysfunction,
- ischemia relatif,
- fibrosis,
- arrhythmia,
- akhirnya heart failure.

## Concentric vs Eccentric Hypertrophy

Concentric hypertrophy:

- pressure overload,
- sarcomeres added in parallel,
- wall thickness meningkat,
- contoh: hypertension, aortic stenosis.

Eccentric hypertrophy:

- volume overload,
- sarcomeres added in series,
- chamber dilation,
- contoh: chronic regurgitation.

![Concentric vs eccentric hypertrophy](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1f_3U_SUje4s53RzwKUT2RkO-PGbyT2mm)

## Valvular Heart Disease — Pendekatan

Pertanyaan utama:

- stenosis atau regurgitation?
- acute atau chronic?
- calcific, rheumatic, degenerative, infectious, atau congenital?

Stenosis → pressure overload.

Regurgitation → volume overload.

## Calcific Aortic Stenosis

Calcific aortic stenosis adalah penyebab penting stenosis aorta pada usia lanjut.

Morphology:

- nodular calcific masses pada cusps,
- commissures umumnya tidak fused pada degenerative calcific stenosis.

Konsekuensi:

- LV pressure overload,
- concentric hypertrophy,
- syncope,
- angina,
- heart failure pada penyakit berat.

## Bicuspid Aortic Valve

Bicuspid aortic valve adalah congenital valve abnormality yang mempercepat calcification dan stenosis.

Clinical significance:

- calcific stenosis lebih dini,
- dapat terkait aortopathy/ascending aortic dilation.

## Mitral Valve Prolapse

Morphology:

- myxomatous degeneration,
- redundant floppy leaflets,
- elongated chordae.

Dapat asimtomatik atau menyebabkan:

- mitral regurgitation,
- arrhythmia,
- endocarditis risk pada kelompok tertentu,
- jarang chordal rupture.

## Rheumatic Fever & Rheumatic Heart Disease

Rheumatic fever adalah immune-mediated complication setelah pharyngeal infection oleh group A Streptococcus.

Acute rheumatic carditis dapat mengenai seluruh lapisan jantung = pancarditis.

Lesi histologis klasik:

- Aschoff bodies,
- Anitschkow cells dengan “caterpillar nuclei”.

Chronic rheumatic heart disease:

- leaflet thickening,
- commissural fusion,
- chordal shortening,
- fish-mouth/buttonhole mitral stenosis.

## Infective Endocarditis

Infective endocarditis = microbial infection of endocardial surface, biasanya valve.

Morphology:

- friable bulky vegetations,
- fibrin + inflammatory cells + microorganisms,
- dapat menghancurkan valve.

Komplikasi:

- septic emboli,
- valve insufficiency,
- abscess,
- immune complex glomerulonephritis.

## Acute vs Subacute Endocarditis

Acute:

- virulent organism,
- dapat menyerang normal valve,
- destructive,
- rapid course.

Subacute:

- organisme lebih rendah virulence,
- sering pada valve abnormal,
- course lebih indolent.

> [!highyield] Exam pearl
> Staphylococcus aureus = klasik acute IE. Viridans streptococci = klasik subacute IE pada damaged valve.

## Nonbacterial Thrombotic Endocarditis

NBTE:

- sterile vegetations,
- small, bland, loosely attached,
- biasanya sepanjang line of closure,
- associated with hypercoagulable states, advanced malignancy, wasting disease.

Dapat menyebabkan systemic emboli.

## Libman–Sacks Endocarditis

Libman–Sacks endocarditis:

- sterile vegetations pada SLE,
- dapat berada pada kedua sisi valve leaflet,
- terkait immune complex injury.

## Cardiomyopathy — Tiga Pola Besar

1. Dilated cardiomyopathy.
2. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.
3. Restrictive cardiomyopathy.

![Dilated vs hypertrophic vs restrictive cardiomyopathy](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1GYZmPYk1-pTviu7xTxB5MEc7X6WKIPcC)

## Dilated Cardiomyopathy

Ciri:

- chamber dilation,
- systolic dysfunction,
- reduced ejection fraction,
- mural thrombi dapat terjadi.

Etiologi:

- genetic,
- myocarditis,
- alcohol/toxin,
- peripartum,
- idiopathic.

Morphology:

- enlarged flabby heart,
- dilation semua chambers terutama ventricles.

## Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy

Sering terkait mutation sarcomeric proteins.

Ciri:

- marked myocardial hypertrophy,
- asymmetric septal hypertrophy klasik,
- diastolic dysfunction,
- dynamic LV outflow obstruction pada sebagian pasien.

Histology:

- myofiber disarray.

> [!klinis] Clinical pearl
> Young athlete + sudden death + family history → hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.

## Restrictive Cardiomyopathy

Ciri:

- impaired ventricular filling,
- ventricular compliance menurun,
- systolic function relatif preserved pada awal.

Penyebab:

- amyloidosis,
- endomyocardial fibrosis,
- hemochromatosis,
- sarcoidosis pada sebagian kasus.

## Myocarditis

Myocarditis = inflammatory injury of myocardium.

Penyebab:

- viral,
- immune-mediated,
- drug/toxin,
- parasitic tertentu.

Morphology bergantung penyebab.

**Classic pathology clue**: lymphocytic myocarditis + myocyte injury pada banyak viral cases.

Chagas disease dapat menyebabkan chronic myocarditis/cardiomyopathy.

## Pericarditis — Pola Morfologi

Serous:

- noninfectious inflammatory states.

Fibrinous:

- uremia,
- MI,
- rheumatic fever.

Purulent/suppurative:

- bacterial infection.

Hemorrhagic:

- malignancy,
- severe infection,
- post-procedure/trauma.

Caseous:

- tuberculosis pada setting tertentu.

## Cardiac Tamponade

Pericardial fluid yang meningkat cepat dapat menghambat ventricular filling → decreased cardiac output.

Penyebab:

- hemopericardium setelah free wall rupture,
- trauma,
- malignancy,
- pericarditis dengan effusion berat.

**Pathology link**: kecepatan akumulasi cairan sama pentingnya dengan volume total.

## Aortic Aneurysm

Aneurysm = abnormal localized dilation of vessel or cardiac wall.

True aneurysm:

- melibatkan seluruh lapisan dinding.

False aneurysm/pseudoaneurysm:

- wall rupture contained by surrounding tissue.

Abdominal aortic aneurysm:

- sangat terkait atherosclerosis,
- paling sering infrarenal.

Thoracic aortic aneurysm:

- dapat terkait hypertension, connective tissue disorders, atau aortitis.

## Aortic Dissection

Aortic dissection terjadi ketika darah masuk ke media melalui intimal tear dan membelah lapisan media.

Faktor risiko:

- hypertension,
- connective tissue disorders seperti Marfan syndrome,
- bicuspid aortic valve/aortopathy pada sebagian pasien.

Komplikasi:

- rupture into pericardium → tamponade,
- rupture into pleural cavity,
- aortic regurgitation,
- branch vessel obstruction.

## Vasculitis — Konsep Ringkas

Vasculitis = inflammation of vessel wall.

Dampak:

- lumen narrowing → ischemia,
- wall weakening → aneurysm/rupture,
- thrombosis.

Klasifikasi sering berdasarkan ukuran vessel:

- large-vessel,
- medium-vessel,
- small-vessel.

Bab sistemik spesifik akan lebih detail pada konteks penyakit terkait; di sini fokus pada konsekuensi patologinya.

## Arteriolosclerosis

Hyaline arteriolosclerosis:

- homogeneous pink hyaline thickening,
- terkait benign hypertension dan diabetes.

Hyperplastic arteriolosclerosis:

- “onion-skin” concentric smooth muscle proliferation,
- khas severe/malignant hypertension.

Fibrinoid necrosis dapat muncul pada severe hypertensive vascular injury.

## Pulmonary Hypertension

Pulmonary hypertension kronik → RV hypertrophy dan dapat berakhir cor pulmonale.

Vascular changes:

- medial hypertrophy,
- intimal fibrosis,
- plexiform lesions pada severe pulmonary arterial hypertension.

## Cardiac Tumors — High-Yield {highyield}

- Metastatic tumors ke jantung lebih sering daripada primary cardiac tumors.

Primary benign klasik pada dewasa:

- atrial myxoma.

Atrial myxoma:

- sering left atrium,
- dapat menyebabkan ball-valve obstruction,
- dapat menimbulkan constitutional symptoms karena IL-6.

Pada anak:

- rhabdomyoma klasik terkait tuberous sclerosis.

## Pattern Recognition Praktikum

**Atherosclerosis**: fibrous cap + necrotic lipid core + cholesterol clefts + foam cells.

**Old MI**: dense collagenous scar menggantikan myocardium.

**Recent MI 1–3 hari**: coagulative necrosis + banyak neutrophils.

**MI 3–7 hari**: macrophages dominan, tissue soft.

**Rheumatic heart disease**: commissural fusion + chordal shortening + fish-mouth mitral valve.

**Rheumatic myocarditis**: Aschoff body + Anitschkow cells.

**Infective endocarditis**: friable vegetations dengan fibrin, inflammation, microorganisms.

**Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy**: myofiber disarray.

**Amyloid restrictive cardiomyopathy**: amorphous extracellular deposits; Congo red positive dengan apple-green birefringence.

## Vignette Clues

- Elderly + crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur + calcified aortic cusps → calcific aortic stenosis.
- Young athlete + sudden death + asymmetric septal hypertrophy → HCM.
- Fever + new murmur + IV drug use + destructive tricuspid vegetation → infective endocarditis.
- Weeks after untreated streptococcal pharyngitis + migratory polyarthritis + carditis → rheumatic fever.
- Chronic HTN + thick LV wall → hypertensive heart disease.
- MI day 4 + sudden hypotension + tamponade → free wall rupture.
- Cancer patient + sterile valve vegetations + emboli → NBTE.
- SLE + sterile vegetations on both sides leaflet → Libman–Sacks.
- Chronic lung disease + RV hypertrophy → cor pulmonale.

## Pembeda Cepat — Pressure vs Volume Overload

Pressure overload:

- hypertension,
- aortic stenosis,
- pulmonary hypertension,
- concentric hypertrophy.

Volume overload:

- mitral/aortic regurgitation,
- shunts,
- eccentric hypertrophy/dilation.

## Pembeda Cepat — Left vs Right Heart Failure

Left:

- pulmonary edema,
- dyspnea,
- orthopnea,
- heart failure cells.

Right:

- peripheral edema,
- JVD,
- hepatomegaly,
- ascites,
- nutmeg liver pada chronic congestion.

- ATHEROSCLEROSIS → vulnerable plaque rupture → thrombosis → MI.
- MI: 1–3 hari neutrophils; 3–7 hari macrophages + rupture; kemudian granulation tissue → scar.
- HTN/aortic stenosis → concentric LVH.
- Regurgitation → eccentric hypertrophy.
- LEFT HF → lungs; RIGHT HF → systemic veins.
- RHEUMATIC = Aschoff + fish-mouth mitral stenosis.
- IE = destructive infected vegetations.
- HCM = myofiber disarray; DCM = dilated chambers; restrictive = impaired filling.
- AAA = atherosclerosis; dissection = HTN/connective tissue.
