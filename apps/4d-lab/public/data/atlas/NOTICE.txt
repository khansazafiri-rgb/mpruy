MEDCOEL Anatomy Live 4D Lab - atlas-derived synthetic dataset
=============================================================

Files in this folder (ct.nrrd, labels.nrrd, skin.glb, anatomy.json, registration.json) were generated by

The full licence and notice of the source models are distributed with the Anatomy Atlas at
/anatomy/viewer/LICENSE, /anatomy/viewer/NOTICE and /anatomy/viewer/models/License.txt.

This is SYNTHETIC educational data (voxelised atlas geometry with textbook CT values). It is not an
image of any person, not patient data, not validated, and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment.
The body surface (skin.glb) is an illustrative approximation.

Changes made: atlas meshes voxelised at 2.5 mm; tissue labels and CT values assigned; an illustrative
body shell derived from the muscle/bone envelope; physiology/landmark parameters fitted to the geometry.
See apps/4d-lab/ATLAS_INTEGRATION_AUDIT.md.
