MEDCOEL Anatomy Live 4D Lab - atlas-derived synthetic dataset
=============================================================

Files in this folder (ct.nrrd, labels.nrrd, skin.glb, anatomy.json, registration.json) were generated by
apps/4d-lab/pipeline/atlas/build_atlas_dataset.mjs from the cleared MEDCOEL Anatomy Atlas models only
(skeletal, muscular, joints, cardiovascular, lymphatic and visceral; see licensing/quarantine-policy.json).
They are an adaptation of BodyParts3D/Z-Anatomy geometry and are distributed under:

  Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Required attributions (verbatim, from the upstream notice of the atlas models):

  - "BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan"
    https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/  (licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/)
  - "Z-Anatomy - The open source atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0"
    https://www.z-anatomy.com/  (licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

Changes made (adaptation notice): the atlas meshes were voxelised at 2.5 mm; tissue labels and textbook
CT values were assigned; an illustrative body shell was derived from the muscle/bone envelope;
physiology, landmark and probe-window parameters were fitted to the geometry.

Licence quarantine (2026-11-09): the following were EXCLUDED from every input of this build and are not
present in any file here, in any form:
  - the whole nervous-system file of the atlas (nervous.glb: unverified "Brainder"/"White matter"
    meshes and CC BY-NC-SA inner-ear components); the spinal cord label is therefore absent;
  - "Kidney - by lissiecowley - CC-BY-NC 4.0" (Kidney.l/.r) and the renal pelvis; the kidney labels are
    marked unavailable in anatomy.json and are not replaced by any other model;
  - the auditory ossicles (precaution: CC BY-NC-SA "Anatomy of the Inner Ear").
Earlier releases of this dataset that contained those inputs are withdrawn.

The source models' licence and notice are distributed with the Anatomy Atlas at /anatomy/viewer/LICENSE,
/anatomy/viewer/NOTICE and /anatomy/viewer/models/License.txt.

This is SYNTHETIC educational data (voxelised atlas geometry with textbook CT values). It is not an
image of any person, not patient data, not validated, and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment.
The body surface (skin.glb) is an illustrative approximation. See apps/4d-lab/ATLAS_INTEGRATION_AUDIT.md.
